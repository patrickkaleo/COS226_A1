Conclusion 1 (Low Contention): 
At 2 threads, MCS is extremely slow. Forcing threads to manage a queue 
structure wastes time when there is almost no traffic. TTAS and CLH are 
much faster here.

Conclusion 2 (Hardware Fit): 
CLH consistently beats MCS. We are running on a standard cache-coherent 
processor, so CLH spinning on a predecessor's memory is perfectly fast. 
The extra complexity of MCS is just dead weight on this specific hardware.

Conclusion 3 (The Small Critical Section): 
At 16 threads, the brute-force TTAS lock surprisingly beats CLH. This is 
because our critical section is microscopic. The processor actually resolves 
the violent TTAS stampede faster than it can build an orderly linked list queue.
